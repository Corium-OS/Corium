**Configuration** specifies every field of the `corium:` schema. **cctl** covers
every command of the management CLI. **Examples** collects annotated documents
for the common node shapes. **Feature support** says what is modelled, what
passes through to k0s, and what is out of scope. **Comparison** covers Talos,
Kairos and the alternatives.

The reasoning behind the architecture lives under
[Decisions](/docs/decisions/).
